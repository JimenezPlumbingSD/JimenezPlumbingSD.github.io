About JPS

San Diego Plumbing — From the Mountains to the Coast

Back in 1989, Chuck Jimenez decided to get his Subcontractor C36 license. No shop front, no ad budget — just a truck, a master's skill, and word-of-mouth reputation built one homeowner at a time across Julian, Ramona, Escondido, Poway, and the greater San Diego county.

Today JPS specializes in what most plumbers can't: full kitchen and bathroom remodel plumbing design, custom new-construction home builds, and commercial restaurant build-outs. Chuck just finished the Ono Hawaiian BBQ in the Mt. Carmel area — the kind of commercial project that demands a master plumber who can design the system, not just install it.

Service work remains the backbone — LP gas line leak detection and repair, slab leak detection, whole-house copper and PEX water repipes, and full sewer repipes. When homeowners in San Diego Country Estates call, JPS answers.

Our Services

Kitchen & Bathroom Remodels

Full plumbing design through fixture installation. From rough-in to final trim, JPS handles the entire plumbing scope on your remodel — $2,800–$5,000 plumbing-only packages.

  • Plumbing layout design
  • Rough-in and rough-in modification
  • Fixture installation (faucets, toilets, tubs, showers)
  • Water line relocation
  • Drain and vent rerouting

Custom Home Plumbing

New construction plumbing design-build. JPS designs the system from the ground up — water distribution, waste and vent, gas lines, and every fixture in the home.

  • Full plumbing system design
  • Under-slab rough-in
  • Water distribution manifold
  • Gas line layout and installation
  • Final trim and fixture setting

Commercial / Restaurant Plumbing

Restaurant and commercial build-outs. Chuck just completed the Ono Hawaiian BBQ in Mt. Carmel — grease traps, backflow, commercial water heaters, and health-code compliance.

  • Restaurant build-outs

  • Commercial water heater systems
  • Health department code compliance

LP Gas Leak Detection & Repair

Gas leaks are not DIY. JPS performs full LP gas line leak detection, pressure testing, and repair. Master plumber sign-off on every gas system.

  • Full gas line leak detection
  • LP and natural gas pressure testing
  • Gas line repair and replacement
  • Appliance gas connection verification
  • Written compliance report

Slab Leaks & Whole-House Repipes

Prevent the $8,000 Repipe

Most whole-house repipes we do could have been avoided with $200 worth of maintenance over ten years. Here is what a master plumber recommends — and when.

Do This Now

  • Know your pipe material. Copper 20+ years old, galvanized steel of any age, or polybutylene (gray plastic) — schedule an inspection. These fail from the inside out. By the time you see a leak, the damage is already under the slab.
  • Check water pressure. Above 80 PSI eats your pipes, water heater, and fixture seals. A $15 gauge from the hardware store tells you in 30 seconds. If it is high, a pressure regulator costs $300 installed — versus thousands in pipe failure.
  • Smell gas? LP gas leaks do not wait. Evacuate, do not flip switches, call (760) 789-3980 once you are clear. JPS performs full LP gas system leak detection, repair, and master plumber sign-off.

Every Year

  • Whole-house plumbing inspection. A licensed plumber walks every fixture, pipe connection, water heater, gas line, and accessible drain. Catches slow leaks, corrosion, and code violations before they become emergencies. JPS-MP Essential members get this included — $179/yr pays for itself the first time it finds a problem.
  • Water heater flush. Sediment buildup kills tanks early and wastes energy. A 15-minute flush adds years to a $1,500 appliance. JPS-MP Plus and Premium members get this free.
  • LP gas system safety check. Verifies all connections, shutoff valves, and appliance regulators. Required for commercial restaurants; smart for every home with gas appliances.

Seasonal

  • Fall: Insulate exposed pipes before first freeze. Disconnect and drain outdoor hose bibs. Check water heater — it works harder in winter and that is when 10-year-old tanks fail.
  • Spring: Check for winter damage. Inspect under-sink connections for slow drips — the kind that rot cabinets and grow mold for months before you notice. Test sump pumps if you have them.
  • Summer: High water usage season. Monitor pressure. If you are on a well or in SD Country Estates, pressure fluctuations are common — a regulator prevents pipe stress.
  • After any seismic activity: San Diego is not earthquake-free. Even a 3.0 can crack a slab pipe or shear a gas connection. If you felt it, call for an inspection.

The math is simple: A JPS-MP membership at $179–$549/yr versus a whole-house repipe at $4,000–$8,000. Prevention is not optional — it is the difference between a scheduled visit and a 2AM emergency that floods your kitchen.

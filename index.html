What we do

Built for the whole job,
not just the easy call.

We specialize in the work most plumbers don't want — new construction, full repipes, tankless conversions, and gas systems.

  • New Construction & Custom Homes

    Full rough-in from ground up. We've been on job sites across San Diego County since 1991 — residential and commercial. We coordinate with your GC and get the plumbing right the first time.

  • Whole-House Repiping

    Complete DWV and domestic water repipes. Copper, PEX, or a combination — scoped to what the house actually needs. We give you the honest answer on materials, not the upsell.

  • Tankless Water Heaters

    Installation, retrofit, and service. Navien, Rinnai, and Noritz. We've run the gas lines, pulled the permits, and done the startup — not just the swap-out.

  • LP Gas & Leak Detection

    Gas line installation and leak detection for rural and fire-zone properties throughout the county. Ramona, Julian, Borrego, Valley Center — we know the territory.

  • Bathroom & Kitchen Remodels

    Full rough-in for remodel projects. We coordinate with your GC on sequencing, get the plumbing right the first time, and keep the schedule moving.

  • Slab Leak Detection & Repair

    Acoustic and thermal detection. Reroute or epoxy lining — we give you the honest answer on which one makes sense for your house, not the one that pays us more.

How it works

Simple process, no runaround.

From first call to final inspection — here's how a job goes with JPS.

  • Step 1 — You call, Chuck picks up

    No dispatcher, no service desk. You reach the plumber directly. Describe the job, get an honest read on scope and timing within the same conversation.

  • Step 2 — Estimate and permit

    Written estimate before work starts. Permits pulled for any job that requires them — no shortcuts that come back on the homeowner at inspection or resale.

  • Step 3 — Work gets done right

    Chuck does the work himself. Not a crew you've never met, not a day-labor sub. The same person who gave you the estimate is the person on the job site.

  • Step 4 — Inspection passed, job closed

    We don't close until it passes. Final walkthrough, inspection sign-off, documentation in your hands. Clean job, no loose ends.

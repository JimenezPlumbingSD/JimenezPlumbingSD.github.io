What We Do

Installation, remodel, and system protection. We do not provide drain cleaning, hydro-jetting, or sewer snaking.

Kitchen & Bath Remodel

Custom trim selection, fixture layout, rough-in, and finish. Chuck walks you through every inspection and helps you pick materials that fit your budget and your house.

Custom Home Plumbing

New construction and custom home rough-in. Copper, PEX, gas lines — built to last from day one. Chuck coordinates with your builder and inspector so nothing gets missed.

Gas Leak Detection & Repair

Natural gas and LP/propane systems. Electronic leak detection, pressure testing, line replacement, and appliance hookups. Critical safety work done right.

Slab Leak Detection & Repair

Electronic detection finds the leak without tearing up your floor. Repair options: spot fix, reroute, or full repipe depending on pipe age and condition.

Whole-House Repipe

Copper or PEX repiping for homes with aging or failing pipes. Most Ramona homes built before 2000 are at or past the expected lifespan of original plumbing.

Tankless Water Heaters

Rinnai, Navien, and Noritz on natural gas and LP/propane. Endless hot water, lower utility bills, 20-year lifespan. Installed from $2,200.

# JPS Plumbing Services - Photo Content Guide

## Required Business Photos

### 1. Team Photos (Priority: High)
**Purpose**: Build trust and personal connection with customers
- **Team Portrait**: Professional group photo of entire team (3-5 people)
  - Format: High-resolution (3000x2000px), well-lit, clean background
  - Attire: Company uniforms with JPS logo
  - Location: Office/storefront or job site
- **Action Shots**: Individual team members working (3-5 photos)
  - Plumber fixing pipes, inspecting water heaters, using equipment
  - Candid shots showing professionalism and expertise
- **Headshots**: Individual portraits for "Meet the Team" section
  - 800x800px, consistent lighting and background

### 2. Service Photos (Priority: High)
**Purpose**: Demonstrate expertise and service quality
- **Emergency Repairs**: Before/after shots of burst pipes, leaks, floods
  - Show damage and completed repair
  - Include 3-5 different emergency scenarios
- **Drain Cleaning**: Hydro-jetting equipment in action
  - Close-ups of equipment and process
  - Before/after pipe interiors (if available)
- **Water Heaters**: Installation and repair photos
  - Tankless water heater installations
  - Traditional water heater maintenance
  - Energy-efficient upgrade examples
- **Leak Detection**: High-tech equipment in use
  - Thermal imaging cameras
  - Acoustic detection devices
  - Moisture meters

### 3. Equipment & Tools (Priority: Medium)
**Purpose**: Showcase professional capabilities
- **Fleet Vehicles**: Company vans with branding
  - Exterior shots with JPS logo and contact info
  - Interior shots showing organized tools
- **Specialized Equipment**:
  - Drain cameras
  - Pipe inspection tools
  - Hydro-jetting machines
  - Trenchless repair equipment
- **Safety Gear**: Team members wearing PPE
  - Gloves, safety glasses, hard hats
  - Clean workspace setup

### 4. Work Environment (Priority: Medium)
**Purpose**: Build credibility and professional image
- **Office/Storefront**: Exterior and interior shots
  - Signage with business hours
  - Waiting area (if applicable)
  - Dispatch/communication setup
- **Warehouse/Storage**: Organized parts and equipment
  - Inventory shelves
  - Workbenches with tools
- **Customer Interaction**: Team members with clients
  - Explaining repairs
  - Providing estimates
  - Completing service calls

### 5. Before/After Gallery (Priority: High)
**Purpose**: Demonstrate value and expertise
- **Pipe Repairs**: 5-10 before/after pairs
  - Corroded pipes → Replaced pipes
  - Clogged drains → Clean drains
- **Bathroom/Kitchen**: Plumbing upgrades
  - Old fixtures → New installations
  - Leaky faucets → Repaired fixtures
- **Water Damage**: Restoration examples
  - Flooded areas → Restored spaces
  - Mold remediation examples

### 6. Community Involvement (Priority: Low)
**Purpose**: Build local brand presence
- **Local Events**: Sponsorships or community service
- **Charity Work**: Plumbing-related community support
- **Educational**: Team teaching plumbing basics

## Technical Specifications

### File Formats
- **Primary**: JPEG (for photos), PNG (for graphics/logos)
- **WebP**: Create optimized WebP versions for faster loading
- **Vector**: SVG for logos and icons

### Image Dimensions
| Usage               | Recommended Size       | Aspect Ratio  | Max File Size |
|---------------------|------------------------|---------------|---------------|
| Hero Image          | 2000x1200px            | 16:9          | 500KB         |
| Service Cards       | 800x600px              | 4:3           | 200KB         |
| Team Photos         | 1200x800px             | 3:2           | 300KB         |
| Before/After        | 1000x800px (each)      | 5:4           | 250KB         |
| Gallery Images      | 1200x900px             | 4:3           | 200KB         |
| Thumbnails          | 400x300px              | 4:3           | 100KB         |

### Optimization Requirements
- **Compression**: Use lossless compression (TinyPNG, ImageOptim)
- **Naming**: Descriptive filenames (e.g., `jps-team-san-diego-2024.jpg`)
- **Alt Text**: Write descriptive alt text for accessibility
- **Color Profile**: sRGB for web consistency

## Photo Shoot Recommendations

**Professional Photographer**: Recommended for:
- Team portraits
- Hero images
- Marketing materials

**DIY Photography**: Suitable for:
- Before/after shots
- Equipment photos
- Candid work photos

**Equipment Needed for DIY**:
- DSLR camera or high-quality smartphone
- Tripod for stable shots
- Good lighting (natural or portable lights)
- Clean backgrounds

## Legal Considerations
- Obtain signed model releases from team members
- Get customer permission for before/after photos of their property
- Ensure no sensitive information is visible in photos
- Maintain consistent branding in all images

Frequently Asked Questions

What does the annual inspection cover?

A licensed JPS master plumber inspects every accessible fixture, pipe connection, water heater, gas line, and drain in your home. You receive a written report with findings, recommendations, and photos. Think of it as a physical for your plumbing system.

Can I upgrade my plan later?

Yes. Upgrade anytime — we prorate the difference. If you start Essential and want Plus six months in, you pay the difference for the remaining six months.

What counts as an emergency service call?

Any after-hours or weekend call that requires immediate attention — burst pipes, active leaks, gas odors, sewer backups, no hot water. Routine maintenance and estimates don't count.

Does this cover commercial properties?

These residential plans are designed for homes. Commercial JPS-MP plans are available with custom pricing based on property size and system complexity. Call for a quote.

What is the AI blueprint estimate?

Premium members can upload blueprints or photos to our AI assistant and receive instant preliminary estimates for remodels, repipes, and new construction. This is powered by Botwave, built by Chuck's son Kyle — a digital tool that helps you plan before you spend.

Can I cancel?

Yes, with 30 days notice. No cancellation fees. You keep your benefits through the end of the paid period.

Frequently Asked Questions

218|
219|
220| What does the annual tankless descaling include? 221|

A licensed JPS master plumber isolates your tankless unit, circulates a food-grade descaling solution through the heat exchanger for 45–60 minutes to dissolve calcium and mineral buildup, then flushes and restores flow. This is mandatory to maintain most manufacturer warranties (Navien, Rinnai, Noritz) and directly impacts energy efficiency and unit lifespan. Standalone, this service runs $200–$300. It's included in every JPS-MP plan, every year.

222|
223|
224| What does the annual plumbing inspection cover? 225|

A licensed JPS master plumber inspects every accessible fixture, pipe connection, water heater, gas line, shutoff valve, and water pressure regulator in your home. You receive a written report with findings, recommendations, and photos. Think of it as a physical for your plumbing system.

226|
227|
228| Do you do drain snaking or sewer clearing? 229|

No — that's not our specialty. JPS focuses on custom home plumbing, tankless systems, remodels, gas lines, and precision work. We are not a rooter service. If you have a main line issue, we'll point you to someone who handles that. What we do, we do right.

230|
231|
232| Do you charge dispatch or trip fees? 233|

JPS-MP members never pay full dispatch or trip fees. Home Preservation members pay 50% of the standard fee. Estate Shield members have all dispatch and diagnostic fees waived entirely. Non-members pay the standard rate, which is waived the moment you enroll.

234|
235|
236| What counts as an emergency service call? 237|

Any after-hours or weekend situation requiring immediate attention — burst pipes, active leaks, LP gas odors, failed water heater, no hot water, sudden pressure loss. Standard maintenance, quotes, and non-urgent repairs don't qualify. When in doubt, call and we'll assess.

238|
239|
240| What are the Remodel Loyalty Credits? 241|

Every year you hold a JPS-MP plan, credits accumulate in your account — $50/year for Home Preservation, $100/year for Estate Shield. They're fully redeemable toward any future bathroom remodel, kitchen rough-in, fixture upgrade, tankless conversion, or addition. Credits roll over year to year and transfer with the membership if you sell.

242|
243|
244| What happens to my membership if I sell my home? 245|

Your JPS-MP plan is fully transferable. When you sell, the remaining term and any unused Remodel Loyalty Credits pass to the new owner — it's a genuine selling point for your listing. Call us when escrow closes and we'll update the account in 10 minutes.

246|
247|
248| What is the Digital Valve Mapping on Estate Shield? 249|

On an Estate Shield visit, we photograph and document every local shutoff valve on your property — water mains, fixture shutoffs, water heater isolations, irrigation feeds — and log them in our system with location descriptions. When you have an emergency at 11pm, you're not hunting behind custom cabinetry in the dark. We know exactly where everything is and so do you.

250|
251|
252| What is the AI blueprint estimate? 253|

Estate Shield members can upload floor plans, blueprints, or jobsite photos to the JPS AI assistant and receive instant preliminary estimates for remodels, repipes, additions, and new construction. It's powered by Botwave — built by Chuck's son Kyle — and it gives you a real number to plan around before you commit to a scope of work.

254|
255|
256| Can I upgrade from Home Preservation to Estate Shield? 257|

Yes. Upgrade anytime — we prorate the difference to the day. If you're six months into a Home Preservation annual plan and want Estate Shield, you pay the pro-rated difference for the remaining six months. No penalties, no restart.

These plans are designed for residential homes. Commercial JPS-MP plans are available with custom pricing based on property size, system count, and service complexity. Call Chuck directly for a quote.

Yes, with 30 days notice. No cancellation fees. You keep your benefits and any Remodel Loyalty Credits through the end of the paid period.

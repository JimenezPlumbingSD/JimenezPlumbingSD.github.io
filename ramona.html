Ramona Homeowner FAQ

My house was built in 1992. Do I need a repipe?

Possibly. Copper from that era is about 30 years old — mid-life to end-of-life depending on water chemistry and pressure. Symptoms to watch: pinhole leaks, green staining on fixtures, low pressure at one fixture while others are fine, or a water bill creeping up for no reason. JPS will assess during a free walkthrough and tell you honestly whether it's time, borderline, or fine for now.

Can you install a tankless water heater on propane?

Yes — and we do it regularly in Ramona. Most Country Estates homes are on LP/propane rather than natural gas. JPS installs Rinnai, Navien, and Noritz units configured for propane. We handle the gas line sizing, venting, and any required permit with San Diego County.

Do you do permits for rural/unincorporated areas?

Yes. San Diego Country Estates is in unincorporated San Diego County, which has different requirements than the City of San Diego. JPS pulls permits through the county for repipes, water heater installs, and gas work. We know the inspection sequence and what the county inspector looks for out here.

What's your service area around Ramona?

Primary: Ramona, San Diego Country Estates, and the 92065 zip code. We also regularly serve Julian, Santa Ysabel, Wynola, and parts of North County San Diego. If you're within 30 minutes of Pine Street, call — we'll come.

Do you charge extra for being in the Country Estates?

No. Standard rates, no drive-time charge, no rural surcharge. We're already here.

What don't you do?

We don't snake drains. We're specialists in repiping, tankless water heaters, LP gas systems, slab leaks, and full plumbing rough-in. If you need drain clearing, call Roto-Rooter. If you need serious plumbing work done right, that's us.
